Do I need prior experience to join the course?

No experience is needed. The course starts with the basics and guides you step by step. You will learn through practical exercises and hands-on projects. By the end, you’ll be confident using AI tools in real scenarios.

How long does it take to complete the course?

Most students finish in 8–12 weeks, depending on how much time they spend each week. The flexible schedule allows you to learn at your own pace. You can also revisit lessons anytime to reinforce your skills.

Will I get a certificate after completing the course?

Yes, a certificate is provided after finishing all lessons and projects successfully. It can be shared with employers or added to your LinkedIn profile. The certificate proves your practical knowledge.

Can I access the course materials anytime?

Yes, all videos, exercises, and resources are available online 24/7 for flexible learning. You can study from anywhere using a computer or tablet. This ensures you never miss a lesson and can review content anytime.
