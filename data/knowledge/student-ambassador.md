# Student Ambassador & Campus Leader Program

## Program Overview
The SARTHI Campus Ambassador Program empowers enthusiastic college students to become technology evangelists and community leaders in their colleges and universities.

## Roles & Responsibilities
- Organize tech seminars, coding workshops, and campus meetups.
- Spread awareness about SARTHI's courses, summer camps, and internships.
- Guide peers on career tracks, tech opportunities, and skill building.
- Coordinate campus registrations and community challenges.

## Perks & Rewards
- Official Campus Ambassador Appointment Letter and Experience Certificate.
- Direct mentorship from SARTHI founders and senior engineers.
- Free access to selected SARTHI courses and workshops.
- Performance stipends, referral bonuses, and priority consideration for SARTHI internships.
- Leadership badges and recognition on the platform leaderboard.
