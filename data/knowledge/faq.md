# SARTHI FAQs & Policies

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q1: Does SARTHI provide a 100% job guarantee?
**Honest Answer:** No educational platform can ethically guarantee a 100% job. SARTHI provides high-quality practical skills, project portfolio building, mock interviews, mentorship, and career readiness guidance that maximize student hiring chances, but we never sell fake guarantees.

### Q2: How are certificates verified?
Every SARTHI course completion certificate and internship credential comes with a unique permanent reference ID (e.g., `TT-INT-2026-0001` or `TT-STU-XXXXXX`) and a public verification link at `https://sarthi-woad.vercel.app/verify` or `https://sarthi-woad.vercel.app/verify/interns/[ID]`.

### Q3: Are classes live or pre-recorded?
Courses feature a blend of comprehensive self-paced structured modules, live hands-on masterclasses, interactive coding exercises, and doubt-clearing sessions.

### Q4: What if I am from a non-CS or non-technical background?
Our courses (especially Python & AI, Power BI, and Summer Camp) start from absolute fundamentals with zero prerequisite knowledge required. Mentors guide learners step by step.

### Q5: What is the refund policy?
SARTHI maintains a student-friendly, transparent policy. If a student encounters genuine technical delivery issues before course access, support can be reached at support@sarthi.in.
