# SARTHI Summer Camp 2026

## Overview
SARTHI Summer Camp is an engaging, beginner-friendly seasonal boot camp specially designed for school students (Classes 6–12), first-year college entrants, and absolute non-tech beginners.

## Key Learning Areas
- **Python From Scratch:** Understand variables, loops, conditionals, and logical problem solving without jargon.
- **Fun Mini-Projects:** Build text games, calculators, simple bots, and interactive quizzes.
- **Creative Tech & AI Exploration:** Introduction to how AI, chatbots, and automation work.
- **Certificate:** Summer Camp Certificate of Completion issued upon submission of final project.

- **Pricing:** Highly accessible promotional fee (starting from ₹11 to ₹199 depending on batch promotions).
- **Format:** Step-by-step interactive sessions, live doubt clearing, and guided exercises.
