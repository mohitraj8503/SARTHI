# SARTHI Industry Internship Program

## Overview
SARTHI offers an industry-grade internship program designed to bridge the gap between college curriculum and real tech startup engineering.

## Available Internship Domains
1. **Web Development & Full Stack Engineering:** Next.js, React, Node.js, Prisma, REST APIs, responsive UI engineering.
2. **Software Development & Python:** Backend services, algorithm design, data pipeline automation.
3. **Digital Marketing & Social Media Growth:** Content marketing, campaign execution, ambassador management, SEO & community building.
4. **Graphic Design & Creative Media:** Brand design, UI mockups, visual media assets.
5. **Video Editing & Reels Production:** Short-form video editing, promotional reels, tutorial screencasts.
6. **Content Creation & Technical Writing:** Technical documentation, developer guides, educational blogging.

## Internship Highlights & Structure
- **Format:** Remote / Hybrid project milestones with mentor code reviews.
- **Duration:** Typically 1 to 2 months with milestone evaluations.
- **Evaluation:** Task-based XP system, sprint deliverables, and code/content review.
- **Certification & Credentials:**
  - Official Verifiable Internship Completion Certificate (with Permanent Intern ID `TTIXXXXXX` / Reference No. `TT-INT-2026-XXXX`).
  - Formal Letter of Recommendation (LOR) signed by Dr. Mukul Pandey (CEO & Founder) for outstanding performers.
- **Verification Portal:** Instantly verifiable publicly at `https://sarthi-woad.vercel.app/verify/interns/[ID]`.
- **How to Apply:** Direct application at `https://sarthi-woad.vercel.app/internship/apply`.
