ML Specialist

Dr. M. Rajeevan (Senior Fellow, MoES)

An ML Specialist guides learners in designing and implementing machine learning models. They help students analyze data, build predictive algorithms, and gain hands-on experience, ensuring practical skills that can be applied to real-world AI challenges.

David’s Profile

Experienced in AI and ML, specializing in building machine learning models, analyzing complex data, and applying AI to real-world challenges. Provides guidance through hands-on projects, helping learners develop practical skills and confidence in using AI effectively.

Teaching Approach

Learning is designed around hands-on practice, blending theory with real exercises. Students work on projects, interactive activities, and problem-solving tasks, ensuring concepts are understood deeply and skills can be confidently applied in AI and ML scenarios.

Benefits of David’s Teaching

  • Gain hands-on experience with real AI projects.
  • Learn practical skills for immediate application.
  • Understand complex concepts in simple ways.
  • Receive guidance from experienced mentors.
  • Build confidence in AI problem-solving.
  • Improve career prospects through advanced skills.

Skills & Expertise

  • Meteorologist-II, Regional Meteorological Centre
  • Data Analysis
  • Model Building
  • AI Fundamentals
  • Neural Networks
